## 申请方式

请将以下材料发送至 **cogcompling at gmail.com**：

- 详细 CV 与本科及硕士阶段成绩单

## English

The CL Lab, led by Hai Hu (Assistant Professor, Department of Language Science and Technology, with joint appointment in the Division of Artificial Intelligence and the Humanities, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University), is recruiting **PhD students** and **research assistants**. We welcome applicants interested in language models, computational linguistics, cognitive modeling, and Chinese NLP. See the [homepage](/) for our research directions.

The lab is well funded and maintains long-term collaborations with institutions including Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Beijing Normal University, and Indiana University. Many CL Lab members have gone on to fully funded PhD programs, e.g., Cognitive Science at Johns Hopkins University, Computational Linguistics at UC San Diego, Psychology at Princeton University, and Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. See [CL Lab](/lab/) for current and former members.

### Who we are looking for

- A background in computer science, linguistics, psychology, foreign languages, Chinese, artificial intelligence, or a related field; Bachelor's or Master's level
- Some programming ability and research experience; publications, submissions, or course papers are a plus
- Passion for research, self-motivation, independent thinking, and a collaborative spirit

### How to apply

Please send the following to **cogcompling at gmail.com**:

- A detailed CV and transcripts (undergraduate and, if applicable, Master's)
- A short personal statement: your background, research interests, and preliminary ideas for PhD research
- One or more samples of previous research writing (course papers, technical reports, submitted papers, etc.), if available

Before a formal admission decision, PhD applicants will typically work with us on a collaborative project for **1–3 months**, so that both sides can get to know each other and make an informed choice; the topic can build on the lab's existing directions or be proposed by the applicant. For research assistants, working arrangements (on-site or remote, payment, authorship, etc.) are negotiable.

Given limited time, if you do not hear back within a week, it may mean the directions do not match at the moment — thank you for your understanding.
